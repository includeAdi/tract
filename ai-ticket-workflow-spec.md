# AI Ticket Workflow Specification

## 1. New
The ticket is created and enters the system. It awaits assignment and assessment.

## 2. Bucketed
The ticket is categorized and placed into the appropriate bucket based on its type and urgency.

## 3. Prioritization
The ticket's priority is evaluated, and it is ranked accordingly to determine its urgency and the order in which it needs to be addressed.

## 4. Triage
The ticket is reviewed by team members to assess its needs and determine the next steps. This may involve gathering more information or delegating tasks.

## 5. Awaiting Information
Additional information is needed before the ticket can proceed. It is flagged, and the responsible parties are notified to provide the necessary details.

## 6. Token Check & Data Check
The relevant tokens and data are verified against criteria established for the workflow. Ensures data integrity and compliance.

## 7. PoC (Proof of Concept)
A proof of concept is developed to demonstrate potential solutions. Feedback is gathered to decide if the project should move forward.

## 8. Token Check & Data Check for Production
A subsequent verification of tokens and data in a production context to ensure everything is ready for deployment.

## 9. Routed to ISS/PRD
The ticket is officially routed to the Infrastructure Support Services or Production department for implementation or resolution.

## 10. Audit Review
The completed ticket undergoes an audit review to evaluate outcomes and ensure compliance with established workflows and standards.
